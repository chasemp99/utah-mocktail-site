The team

The people behind the bar at every Utah Mocktail Co. event.

Founder and lead bartender

A drink for everyone at the party.

I started Utah Mocktail Co. after years behind the bar, watching the same thing happen at every event: hosts had to choose between a casual soda setup or a traditional bar where mocktails were an afterthought.

There was almost nothing for people who wanted a premium, alcohol-free experience, so I built it. As a BYU marketing student with a background in Utah Valley hospitality, I wanted to create beverage experiences that feel sophisticated and inclusive, where no one has to opt out.

From a thirty-person dinner to a four-hundred-guest reception, every event gets the same thing: handcrafted drinks, premium ingredients, beautiful presentation, and genuine hospitality.

Jacob, founder of Utah Mocktail Co.
