Questions

Are all the drinks alcohol-free?

Yes. Every drink we serve is alcohol-free, so the whole room can have one.

What if more guests come than the package covers?

Extra guests are $5 each. Tell us your best guess when you reserve your date, and we'll suggest the package that fits.

Do you travel outside Utah County?

Yes. We serve Salt Lake, Davis, Weber, Summit, Wasatch and Southern Utah, and we'll talk about destination events. Travel can change the final price.

Can we have a drink that isn't on the menu?

Yes. We can create one around your colors or theme. The Luxe Dry Bar package includes four custom drinks and a planning call before the event.

Meet our team

The people behind the bar

Our team is led by Jacob, our founder and lead bartender, who spent years making craft cocktails before starting the company. Every bartender we send is trained to make each drink the same careful way.
