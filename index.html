How it works

Three easy steps from first message to last pour.

  1. 01

    Reach Out

    DM us on Instagram or fill out the form below. Tell us a little about your event, and let’s start dreaming up something special. A member of our team will reach out to you shortly.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    We’ll connect over FaceTime or meet in person to find the perfect package for your event. From custom mocktails to the finishing touches, we’ll tailor every detail to your vision. We’ll provide a free, no-obligation quote and work with you to create the perfect setup for your event!

  3. 03

    Sit back & sip

    Once the details are set, you can check one more thing off your list!

    We’ll take care of everything from start to finish, bringing the drinks, fresh ingredients, and real glassware, setting up the bar, serving your guests, and handling all the cleanup. You focus on celebrating and making memories—we’ll handle the rest!
