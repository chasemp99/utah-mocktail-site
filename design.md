# Utah Mocktail Co. design rules (draft 9, 2026-10-27)

Direction: Jake's old site (utah-mocktail-site.vercel.app) look, kept on the new site's layout. Cream pages, dark brown text and buttons, gold accents. Jake asked for this scheme back at the 10/22 meeting (feedback-2026-10-22.md). What Jake picked from the old site is in old-site-review/notes.md.

Layout still follows outwestelopements.com: full-width video hero, short centered text, big photos between sections. The centered logo with the menu split around it comes from kateelizabethevents.com.

## Colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| bg | #f3ecda | page background (old site's cream) |
| bg-2 | #ede3cc | panels (wedding clip, Everything included) |
| card | #f8f3e7 | cards and the form card |
| fg | #2a2418 | text, buttons, the one dark "Why us" section |
| fg-soft | #645a47 | secondary text |
| gold | #846a39 | italic accent words, labels, links (4.6:1 on bg) |
| trim | #b89a64 | thin lines, dots, step numbers, menu card trim |

## Type
- Headings: Cormorant Garamond 500, italic gold for one or two key words.
- Body: Inter. Small spaced capitals for labels and buttons, as on the old site.
- Buttons: dark brown pills with spaced capitals.

## Motion (all off with "reduce motion")
1. The header sits clear over the hero video, then turns solid once you scroll.
2. Drink names scroll slowly in a line between thin gold rules.
3. Drink cards lift, get a thicker gold border and zoom the photo on hover.
4. Photos ease up into place once when they come into view.

## Home page order
Hero video, facts, "Nobody left out of the toast", drink names strip, wide photo, Why us (dark, with the candid bartender photo), venues line, wedding clip (opens Instagram), corporate and parties, 4 signature drink cards plus the full menu card, How it works (3 steps, Chase's wording), Everything included grid, Packages teaser, date form card, team card. Prices live on packages.html (swipe-through cards).

## Avoid
Invented reviews; words like elevated, unforgettable, seamless; fade-in on every text block; scroll hijacking.
