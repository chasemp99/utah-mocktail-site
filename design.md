# Utah Mocktail Co. design rules (draft 8)

Direction: a light, romantic wedding look. Warm white pages, blush panels and a deep berry color taken from the pink and berry drinks in Jacob's photos. Layout follows outwestelopements.com (Chase's reference): full-width video hero, short centered text, big photos between sections.

Why the change from draft 6: Chase said the cream, brown and gold looked AI-made. The research (research/service-website-design-playbook-2026-10.md) also flags cream + serif as an AI cluster, and Cormorant Garamond as a font Claude picks for every site.

Draft 8: Chase shared a screenshot of kateelizabethevents.com (Utah wedding planner). Taken from it: an all-light page (no dark sections), centered logo with the menu split around it in small spaced capitals, thin rose lines under centered headings, italic text links, and the "meet the founder" card set over a soft full-width photo. Not copied: their words, photos or script logo.

## Type
- Headings: Bodoni Moda, weight 400, with italic for one or two key words. Bridal-magazine feel.
- Body: Public Sans.
- Labels in sentence case. No all-caps eyebrows.

## Motion (3 effects only, all off with "reduce motion")
1. The header sits clear over the hero video, then turns solid once you scroll.
2. The drink photo strip slides sideways as you scroll down.
3. Photos ease up into place once when they come into view.

## Keep
The hero video, "Nobody left out of the toast", "our team" wording, package cards, a home page that carries everything, the date form last.

## Avoid
Invented reviews; words like elevated, unforgettable, seamless; identical icon cards; fade-in on every text block; scroll hijacking.
